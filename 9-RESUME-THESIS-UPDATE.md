## 4. Thesis — card copy (final)

> **"The Second Great Game: the U.S. and China in the Indo-Pacific"** — Undergraduate thesis, Ewha Womans University (Nov 2024). What does the 21st century's defining rivalry look like when set beside the 19th century's? Michelle's thesis reads the U.S.–China contest in the Indo-Pacific — the Belt and Road Initiative, Gwadar and Hambantota ports, South China Sea island-building, FOIP, the Quad, and AUKUS — against the Great Game between Britain and Russia. Its comparative finding: the instruments of power have shifted from territorial conquest to economic diplomacy, infrastructure, and alliance-building, but the underlying motives — control of strategic regions, with smaller states caught in between — remain strikingly constant.
