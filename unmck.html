Since 2024

UN Memorial Cemetery in Korea

Fundraising Project Manager, Jun 2024-present · Busan, Korea

Visit project site ↗

At a glance

  • Mentors 8 students from Daegu, Seoul, St. Louis, Salt Lake City, and Detroit for the annual UN Memorial Ceremony
  • Interviewed U.S. Rear Admiral Neil Koprowski to help publicize the annual ceremony
  • Designed, funded, and distributed 100 “Freedom Isn't Free” T-shirts: the first round funded tributes on 20 graves, and a student team with a donation site added 31 more so far
  • Prepares presentation materials U.S. students use to share the story of UN sacrifices at their schools
  • Reported the project: “Honoring the Heroes” (Leaders Times, 2024)

I wanted my gratitude to extend beyond those buried at this world's only United Nations cemetery to those still serving. The following month, I joined Soldiers' Angels.
