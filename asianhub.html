AsianHub

Translation Volunteer (ITT Level 2 certified), Aug 2019-present · Seoul, Korea · asianhub.kr ↗

At a glance

  • Translates school notices, newsletters, bank regulations, and educational forms (KR→EN) for immigrants and multicultural families
  • ITT Level 2 certificate: accompanies and interprets in person for migrant mothers at clinics and hospitals
  • Reviews the folktale series “Storybooks in the Language of Mom's Home Country” for translation accuracy and cultural sensitivity
  • Records English audiobooks for community reading programs

Early experiences translating stories and academic texts and interacting with multicultural families, especially migrant mothers in Korea, proved to me how essential language access and sensitivity to cultural nuance are to their meaningful participation in education, public services, and community life. This understanding inspired me to broaden my efforts, from working with the Mission Center for Migrants to eventually co-developing Jurio as a bilingual legal-information resource.
